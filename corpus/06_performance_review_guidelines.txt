Performance Review Guidelines
Version 2.0 | People & Culture

---

PURPOSE

Meridian's performance review process is designed to ensure employees receive clear, consistent, and constructive feedback, and that strong performance is recognised and rewarded. Reviews also identify development needs and inform promotion and compensation decisions.

REVIEW CYCLE

Meridian conducts two formal reviews per year:
- Mid-Year Review: July (covers January–June)
- Annual Review: January (covers the full calendar year, sets objectives for the year ahead)

In addition, all employees have monthly 1-on-1s with their line manager. These are informal and not documented on the system, but are expected to be substantive.

THE RATING SCALE

Performance is assessed on a 5-point scale:
1 – Below Expectations: Performance does not meet the minimum requirements of the role. A performance improvement plan (PIP) is triggered.
2 – Developing: Performance meets some but not all expectations. Active coaching required.
3 – Meets Expectations: Solid, reliable performance. Objectives are met consistently.
4 – Exceeds Expectations: Performance is consistently above what is required. Delivers additional impact.
5 – Outstanding: Exceptional performance with measurable business impact beyond the scope of the role.

The expected distribution is approximately 10% at 5, 25% at 4, 50% at 3, 12% at 2, and 3% at 1. Line managers must calibrate with their peers to avoid grade inflation.

OBJECTIVE SETTING

Objectives are set collaboratively between the employee and their line manager at the start of each year and reviewed at mid-year. Objectives must be SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound). Typically 4–6 objectives are set per employee; no more than 8.

Objectives can be amended during the year if business priorities shift, but changes must be documented and agreed by both parties in the performance system.

THE REVIEW PROCESS

Step 1: Employee completes a self-assessment in Meridian Connect (due 2 weeks before the review meeting).
Step 2: Line manager completes their assessment and rating.
Step 3: Line manager submits for calibration with their peer group (moderated by the department head).
Step 4: Review meeting between employee and line manager to discuss ratings and feedback.
Step 5: Employee signs off (agreement or disagreement noted). Documents are retained for 5 years.

PROMOTION AND COMPENSATION LINK

Salary increases and bonuses are linked to the annual review rating. Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for merit increases in the April salary review. Employees rated 1 are ineligible for any increase until the PIP is resolved.

Promotions are discussed at the annual review but may be proposed at any time during the year. A promotion requires a 4 or 5 rating in the most recent review and endorsement from the department head.

PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS

A PIP is initiated for any employee rated 1. The PIP sets specific, measurable improvement goals over a defined period (typically 30–90 days). The employee's progress is monitored weekly by the line manager. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in further disciplinary action.
