WELCOME TO MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES

This guide covers everything a new employee needs for their first 30 days. Please read it in full during your first week and refer to it whenever you have questions about standard procedures.

YOUR FIRST DAY

On arrival, report to Reception on the Ground Floor and ask for your designated onboarding buddy. Your buddy is a peer-level colleague assigned to help you navigate your first two weeks. HR will have communicated their name to you in advance.

You will receive:
- A temporary access badge (valid 7 days, replaced by your permanent badge after ID verification)
- A laptop pre-configured with standard software
- A printed floor map and department directory
- Access credentials to your email and the internal portal (Meridian Connect)

IT SETUP

Your laptop will have core software installed: email client, Slack, Confluence, Jira, and standard productivity tools. Submit a ticket to the IT Helpdesk (helpdesk@meridian.tech) for any additional software requests. Non-standard software requires line manager approval before IT will install it.

Your corporate email follows the format: firstname.lastname@meridian.tech

30-DAY ONBOARDING SCHEDULE

Week 1: Department orientation, system access setup, buddy introductions, mandatory compliance training (Information Security, Code of Conduct, Data Privacy). All four courses must be completed within 5 business days of start date.

Week 2: Role-specific training with your line manager. Review of current team projects and priorities. Introduction meetings with key cross-functional stakeholders.

Week 3–4: Begin contributing to assigned work. First 1-on-1 with line manager. Review personal development objectives for the first 90 days.

PROBATION PERIOD

All new hires are subject to a 90-day probation period. During probation, your performance will be assessed against the objectives set in week 3. A formal mid-probation check-in occurs at day 45. Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by your line manager with HR copied.
