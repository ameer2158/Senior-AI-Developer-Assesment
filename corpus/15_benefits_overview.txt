OVERVIEW

This document summarises the benefits available to permanent Meridian employees. Most benefits activate after successful completion of the 90-day probation period. Contract and temporary workers are not eligible for the benefits listed here unless explicitly stated in their contract.

HEALTH AND WELLBEING

Private Medical Insurance: Meridian provides comprehensive PMI through Bupa for all permanent employees and their immediate dependants (spouse/partner and children under 18). Cover includes inpatient, outpatient, mental health, physiotherapy, and dental (up to £500/year). Enrol via Meridian Connect within 30 days of probation completion; late enrolment requires medical underwriting.

Mental Health Support: All employees have access to an Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) providing 24/7 confidential counselling (up to 8 sessions per year) and a general wellbeing helpline. Access details are on Meridian Connect under Benefits.

Gym Membership: Meridian offers a subsidised gym membership (up to £50/month) via the Benefits Portal. Participating gym chains include PureGym, Anytime Fitness, and Nuffield Health.

Cycle to Work Scheme: Tax-efficient bicycle and equipment purchases up to £2,000 via salary sacrifice. Available year-round; enrol via the Benefits Portal.

FINANCIAL

Pension: Meridian contributes 6% of basic salary to the pension scheme (Scottish Widows). Employees must contribute a minimum of 4%. Total minimum contribution is 10%. Employees may increase their contribution via the Benefits Portal at any time.

Life Assurance: 4x annual salary paid as a lump sum to named beneficiaries in the event of death in service. Nominate beneficiaries on Meridian Connect.

Income Protection: After 4 weeks of continuous sick leave, employees become eligible for Income Protection Insurance covering 75% of salary for up to 2 years (assessed by the insurer).

Annual Salary Review: Salary is reviewed annually in April. Increases are linked to performance rating (see Performance Review Guidelines). Cost-of-living adjustments are considered separately by the Remuneration Committee.

Discretionary Bonus: Eligible roles participate in an annual discretionary bonus scheme. Bonuses are paid in March based on company performance and individual performance rating. Details are communicated separately by Finance.

FAMILY

Enhanced Maternity / Paternity Leave: See the Leave & Time Off Policy for full entitlements.

Childcare: Meridian participates in the government's Tax-Free Childcare scheme. The company does not operate its own nursery or childcare voucher scheme.

LIFESTYLE AND FLEXIBILITY

Flexible Working: Hybrid model (minimum 2 days in office). Fully flexible hours are available in some roles by agreement with the line manager.

Volunteering Days: 2 paid volunteering days per year for approved charitable activities.

Employee Discount Platform: Access to retail, travel, and entertainment discounts via Perkbox (details on Meridian Connect).

Sabbatical: Employees with 5+ years of service are eligible to apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 1–3 months. Applications require 3 months' notice and department head approval. Sabbaticals are not guaranteed and depend on business needs.

CHANGES TO BENEFITS

Meridian reviews the benefits package annually. Changes are communicated with at least 3 months' notice. Employees impacted by a material reduction in benefits will be individually consulted.
