ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees are entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per year, inclusive of UK public holidays (8 days). This equals 20 days of discretionary annual leave. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement.

Leave entitlement increases with tenure:
- 0–2 years: 28 days (20 + 8 public holidays)
- 2–5 years: 30 days (22 + 8 public holidays)
- 5+ years: 33 days (25 + 8 public holidays)

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. A maximum of 5 days may be carried over into the following year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Requests to carry over more than 5 days require People & Culture approval and must be justified by business need.

REQUESTING LEAVE

Submit all leave requests via Meridian Connect at least 2 weeks before the intended start date (4 weeks for periods of 5 or more consecutive days). Leave is not confirmed until approved by the line manager in the system. Do not make non-refundable travel bookings before approval is confirmed.

Managers must respond to leave requests within 3 business days. Leave may be declined if it conflicts with a critical project deadline, leaves the team short-staffed below minimum cover, or falls within a blackout period (announced at least 6 weeks in advance by department heads, typically around major releases or financial close).

SICK LEAVE

Meridian operates a self-certification system for sick leave of up to 5 consecutive working days. For absences of 6 days or more, a fit note (GP certificate) is required and must be submitted to People & Culture on return.

Meridian pays full salary during sick leave for:
- 0–1 year of service: up to 4 weeks
- 1–3 years of service: up to 8 weeks
- 3+ years of service: up to 12 weeks

After the paid period, statutory sick pay (SSP) applies at the current government rate. Long-term sickness (4+ weeks) triggers a referral to occupational health.

PARENTAL LEAVE

Maternity leave: up to 52 weeks (26 ordinary + 26 additional). Meridian pays enhanced maternity pay of 90% of average weekly earnings for the first 16 weeks, then statutory maternity pay for the remaining eligible period.

Paternity/partner leave: 2 weeks paid at full salary, taken within 56 days of the birth or adoption placement. An additional 2 weeks unpaid is available.

Shared parental leave is available and follows the statutory framework.

COMPASSIONATE LEAVE

Up to 5 days paid leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 2 days for extended family (grandparent, in-law). Additional unpaid leave may be agreed at the manager's discretion.

OTHER LEAVE TYPES

Jury service: full pay for the duration (claim reimbursement from the court, return difference to Meridian).
Volunteering: 2 days per year paid leave for approved volunteering activity.
Study leave: as per Learning & Development Policy.
Emergency dependant leave: reasonable unpaid time off to deal with an emergency involving a dependant, per statutory rights.
