MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES
Travel & Expense Policy
Version 2.4 | Finance

---

SCOPE

This policy covers all business-related travel and expenses incurred by employees on behalf of Meridian Technologies. Personal expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

PRE-APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

All travel involving flights, overnight accommodation, or total estimated cost over £200 requires pre-approval via the Expense Portal before booking. Approval is required from the line manager for trips under £1,000 total, and from the department head for trips £1,000 and above.

Employees must book travel at least 14 days in advance where possible to access the lowest fares. Last-minute bookings above £500 must include a written business justification.

FLIGHTS

Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours. Business class may be used for flights over 6 hours, subject to department head approval. Premium economy is acceptable for flights between 4–6 hours without additional approval.

Use Meridian's preferred travel agency (TravelDesk, accessible via Meridian Connect) where possible. Bookings outside TravelDesk must have a cost comparison to demonstrate value.

ACCOMMODATION

Hotel bookings must not exceed £150 per night in standard UK cities and £200 per night in London or any international major city. Exceptions require finance approval. Employees must not stay at hotels more than one star above the budget cap without written authorisation.

GROUND TRANSPORT

Use public transport or pre-approved ride-share (Uber Business account) for local travel. Personal car use is reimbursed at HMRC approved rate (currently 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles, 25p per mile thereafter). Parking at airports or venues is reimbursable; parking fines are not.

MEALS AND DAILY SUBSISTENCE

Daily meal allowances:
- Breakfast: £12 (only if travel requires departure before 07:00)
- Lunch: £15
- Dinner: £30 (only when travel extends past 19:00)

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of an approved client entertainment event (see Client Entertainment below).

CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT

Client meals and entertainment require pre-approval from the department head. The purpose, client name, and attendees must be documented in the expense claim. The total per-person limit for client meals is £80 excluding VAT.

EXPENSE SUBMISSION

All expenses must be submitted via the Expense Portal within 30 days of being incurred. Receipts are mandatory for any item over £10. Expenses submitted without receipts will not be reimbursed. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle after approval.

Repeated late submissions or claims without receipts may result in the employee's reimbursement rights being suspended pending a review by Finance.

CORPORATE CARD

Employees issued a corporate card must use it for all travel bookings and business expenses where possible. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in the Expense Portal monthly by the 5th of the following month. Failure to reconcile on time will be escalated to the line manager.
