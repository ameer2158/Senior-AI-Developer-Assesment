MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES
Code of Conduct
Version 1.8 | People & Culture & Legal

---

OUR COMMITMENT

Meridian Technologies is committed to maintaining a workplace that is safe, respectful, and inclusive. This Code of Conduct sets out the behaviours expected of every employee, contractor, and partner acting on behalf of Meridian.

RESPECT AND INCLUSION

All individuals are to be treated with dignity and respect regardless of age, gender, gender identity, ethnicity, national origin, religion or belief, disability, sexual orientation, marital status, or any other protected characteristic.

Harassment — including verbal, physical, and digital forms — is strictly prohibited. This includes unwanted comments about appearance, repeated unwanted contact, and the sharing of offensive material. Meridian operates a zero-tolerance policy: substantiated harassment findings result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

Discrimination in hiring, promotion, compensation, or any other employment decision based on a protected characteristic is illegal and against Meridian policy. Any employee who believes they have been discriminated against should contact People & Culture or use the confidential Speak Up Line (0800 123 4567).

CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

Employees must not engage in outside business activities that compete with Meridian, or that could create a conflict of interest. If you hold shares in a competitor, sit on an external board, or have a financial relationship with a supplier, disclose this in writing to People & Culture annually.

Gifts from suppliers, clients, or partners must be declared if they exceed £50 in value. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) must never be accepted regardless of value.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees have access to confidential information about Meridian's business, clients, and colleagues. This information must not be shared externally without authorisation. Confidentiality obligations survive the end of employment.

Employees must not use Meridian's confidential information for personal gain or disclose it to future employers. Non-disclosure agreements apply where specifically signed, but the general duty of confidentiality applies to everyone.

HONEST AND ACCURATE REPORTING

Financial records, timesheets, expense claims, and any other business records must be accurate and complete. Falsifying records — including expense fraud, false timekeeping, or misrepresenting results — is a serious misconduct matter and may result in criminal referral.

SOCIAL MEDIA

Employees may discuss their professional role publicly but must not disclose confidential business information, make statements that could be mistaken for official Meridian communications, or post material that is defamatory, harassing, or discriminatory. Use of the Meridian logo or brand in personal posts requires written approval from Marketing.

ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY

Meridian is committed to reducing its environmental impact. Employees are expected to minimise paper use, participate in office recycling programmes, and consider environmental impact when making travel decisions (video calls over flights where feasible).

REPORTING CONCERNS

Concerns about potential breaches of this Code can be raised with a line manager, People & Culture, or the Speak Up Line (0800 123 4567, anonymous reports accepted). Meridian prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a genuine concern in good faith.
