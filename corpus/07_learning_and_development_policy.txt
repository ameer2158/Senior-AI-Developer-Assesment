COMMITMENT TO GROWTH

Meridian believes that continuous learning is essential to individual and organisational success. This policy sets out the support available to employees for professional development and how to access it.

ANNUAL LEARNING BUDGET

Every permanent employee who has completed their probation period is allocated an annual Learning & Development (L&D) budget of £1,500. This budget is use-it-or-lose-it and resets on 1 January each year. It may be used for:
- External training courses and workshops
- Professional certifications and examination fees
- Relevant conferences (registration fee only; travel and accommodation are covered separately under the Travel & Expense Policy)
- Books, online learning subscriptions (e.g. Coursera, Pluralsight, O'Reilly), and other self-study materials

The budget cannot be used for hobbies unrelated to the role, or for courses taken before approval is granted.

HOW TO ACCESS THE BUDGET

Submit a learning request via Meridian Connect (People & Culture → Learning & Development → Submit Request). Include the course/resource name, provider, cost, and a brief justification explaining how it supports your role or development objectives. Your line manager approves requests up to £500. Requests above £500 require both line manager and department head approval. Turnaround is typically 5 business days.

INTERNAL TRAINING PROGRAMMES

Meridian runs the following internal programmes at no cost to the employee's L&D budget:
- Meridian Leadership Academy: 12-week programme for senior individual contributors and new managers (twice yearly intake, nomination by department head)
- Technical Skills Series: Monthly half-day workshops covering engineering, data, and analytics topics (open to all)
- Lunch & Learn: Weekly informal sessions hosted by employees sharing expertise (voluntary, recorded)
- Mandatory Compliance Training: Information Security, Data Privacy, Code of Conduct, and Anti-Bribery — all must be renewed annually

STUDY LEAVE

Employees sitting professional examinations are entitled to up to 2 days paid study leave per examination, taken in the 5 working days before the exam date. This requires line manager approval and does not count against annual leave.

PROFESSIONAL MEMBERSHIPS

Meridian will fund one professional membership per employee per year (e.g. BCS, CIPD, CIMA, IEEE) up to £250. This is separate from the L&D budget. Submit via the same request form, selecting "Professional Membership" as the category.

MENTORING PROGRAMME

Meridian operates a voluntary internal mentoring programme. Employees can register as a mentor, a mentee, or both via Meridian Connect. Matches are made quarterly by the L&D team. Mentoring meetings are considered working time and should not be scheduled to exceed 2 hours per month.

TRAINING AGREEMENT

For training costing over £2,000, a training agreement applies. If the employee leaves Meridian within 12 months of completing the training, they are required to repay a pro-rated portion of the cost (100% within 3 months, 50% within 3–12 months). This is waived if the departure is due to redundancy.
